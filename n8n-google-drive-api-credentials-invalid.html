Google Drive node reports API credentials are invalid in n8n

Error: API credentials are invalid · Node: Google Drive

What this error actually means

The "API credentials are invalid" error in n8n is a common issue when trying to authenticate with a Google Drive account. This error occurs when the credentials provided to the Google Drive node are incorrect or not properly configured.

When you encounter this error, it's essential to understand that it's not just a matter of retrying the connection or adjusting some settings. The error is a clear indication that there's a problem with the credentials themselves.

The three most common causes

After analyzing numerous cases of this error, we've identified three primary causes:

  1. Incorrect Client ID or Client Secret: Make sure you've entered the correct Client ID and Client Secret for your Google Drive account. These values can be obtained from the Google Cloud Console.
  2. Missing or Incorrect Scopes: Ensure that the scopes you've selected in the Google Drive node match the ones required by your application. You can find the required scopes in the Google Cloud Console.
  3. Expired or Revoked Credentials: Check if your credentials have expired or been revoked. This can happen if you've changed your password or if the credentials were manually revoked.

Step-by-step fix

To resolve the "API credentials are invalid" error, follow these steps:

  1. Verify your credentials: Double-check that you've entered the correct Client ID and Client Secret. Make sure they match the ones displayed in the Google Cloud Console.
  2. Configure scopes correctly: Ensure that the scopes you've selected in the Google Drive node match the ones required by your application. You can find the required scopes in the Google Cloud Console.
  3. Update your credentials: If your credentials have expired or been revoked, update them in the Google Cloud Console and re-enter them in the Google Drive node.

Here's an example of how to update your credentials in a Code node:

const credentials = {
  "client_id": "YOUR_CLIENT_ID",
  "client_secret": "YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET",
  "scopes": ["https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive"]
};

// Update the credentials in the Google Drive node
n8n.nodes.GoogleDrive.updateCredentials(credentials);

Replace YOUR_CLIENT_ID and YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET with your actual credentials.

Prevent it from coming back

To prevent this error from occurring in the future, make sure to:

  • Store your credentials securely using a secrets manager or an environment variable.
  • Regularly review and update your credentials to ensure they haven't expired or been revoked.
  • Use a Code node to update your credentials programmatically, so you don't have to manually update them in the Google Drive node.

When to escalate

If you've followed the steps above and still encounter the "API credentials are invalid" error, it's likely that there's a more complex issue at play. In this case, consider escalating the issue to your organization's IT department or a Google Cloud support specialist. They can help you troubleshoot the issue and provide further assistance.
